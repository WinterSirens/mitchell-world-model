## Post Body

The genie has one rule.

He can only grant what you say out loud. Not what you mean. Not what you picture. Whatever leaves your mouth, that's what shows up.

You've been planning this wish since you were seven.

"I want a new car."

The smoke clears.

In your driveway sits a Toyota Corolla.

Beige. Reasonable mileage. Power windows. Technically, exactly what you asked for.

The dream car is still parked in your head, where the genie was never going to find it.

AI is the same. It can't read your mind either.

Where do you find the biggest struggle in getting the output you actually want?
