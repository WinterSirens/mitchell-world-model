**Trigger:** Notes I wanted to add as a part of how I make decisions to my Therapy session.

the biggest thing I struggle with is wanting validation. I am seeing that I give but it doesn't seem like a lot of people give back. My therapist gave me some great advice I don't want to forget: 

*"Givers Sleep well. Takers Eat well"*

I'm still processing that quote and found that I would rather sleep well than eat well. Meaning, when you sleep well, you are at peace and you are feeling happy. If you eat well you can, in that moment, feel good but you may not sleep well because of the worries and stress you have about life. 

That is my current takeaway from that moment. A good leader belongs no where but can fit in anywhere. This is what I would like to keep in mind as I continue to work towards:

1. Is this helping me grow?
2. Am I contributing with my efforts. 

Today was a great session.
