# My Scripture Study Insights

## Core shift in why I study

Studying the scriptures is not mainly about gathering material to sound convincing,  
but about letting Christ become convincing in me.

The power to influence and lead others flows from who I am becoming through the word,  
not just from what I can say.

### Old mindset → New mindset

- Old: “I read so I have something to say.”  
- New: “I read so Christ can change me, and then my words carry His power.”

***

## Alma 32:28 – Letting the word swell

Alma 32:28 teaches that the word, when given place in the heart, can swell, enlarge the soul, and become “delicious” over time.

Growth is less about dramatic moments and more like tending a plant:  
consistent, quiet care that leads to real growth.

Key idea:  
It’s not the novelty that matters most;  
it’s the consistency of taking care of a plant that grows.

***

## D&C 11:21 / 112:21 – Obtain the word first

D&C 11:21 and 112:21 show that the Lord invites me to obtain His word first,  
and then my tongue is loosed and I’m given power to reach others.

Influence comes after personal conversion, not before.

This means:  
Scripture study *powers* and *fuels* me for influence,  
rather than just being a preparation step.

***

## How this shapes my daily desire

When scripture study feels flat or not “delicious,”  
I can:

- Bring one specific real‑life need to the Lord each day.  
- Ask, *“How does this passage help me come closer to Christ in that area?”*  
- Let the word swell slowly—less about novelty, more about faithful small steps.

Over time, this daily pattern:

- Nourishes my desire to open the scriptures.  
- Lets Christ become more visible and convincing in me.  
- Naturally gives me power to influence and serve others in ways that actually matter.
