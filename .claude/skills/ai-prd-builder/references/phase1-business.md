# Phase 1: Business Understanding

## Key Questions to Address

### Business Objectives
- What problem are we solving and why now?
- Who are the primary stakeholders and decision owners?
- What outcomes matter most (cost reduction, efficiency, risk mitigation, satisfaction)?
- What are explicit success criteria with target values?

### Cognitive Requirements
- Why an AI solution vs. non-AI alternatives?
- What level of autonomy is acceptable (assistive, human-in-the-loop, fully automated)?
- What inputs will the system consume and outputs must it produce?
- What latency, throughput, and availability do users expect?

### Situation Assessment
- What team resources and skills are needed?
- What technology resources are required?
- What is the delivery timeline with milestones?
- What are the key constraints and dependencies?

### Performance Targets
- Model/system performance goals (accuracy, response time, resolution rate)
- Business KPIs to improve (cost savings, time reduction, satisfaction scores)
- Technology KPIs (availability, latency, throughput)

### Trustworthy AI
- What ethical considerations apply?
- What are potential failure modes and contingencies?
- What human-in-the-loop requirements exist?
- How will bias be identified and mitigated?

## Go/No-Go Criteria

**Business feasibility:**
- Clear problem definition
- Sponsor commitment and budget
- Defined ROI/impact expectations

**Data feasibility:**
- Required data identified and accessible
- Sufficient quantity and quality
- Acceptable privacy/compliance posture

**Technical/Execution feasibility:**
- Skills and resources available
- Timeline achievable
- Deployment environment ready
