# Five-Year Strategic Plan: AI SHIFT to $5M by 40
## Goal and time constraint
The goal is to reach a $5M annual revenue run-rate by August 2031, when Mitchell turns 40. The starting point is early-stage: PLF revenue is not yet validated, the email list is at 0, the lead magnet is not live, the Marily workshop path is the leading distribution opportunity, and the Capstone Facilitator role is a $3K/cohort starting wedge.
The core strategic truth: the current PLF launch and Capstone role cannot directly become $5M/year as-is. They can become the proof engine for a larger business built around AI SHIFT as a practical training system for professionals, teams, bootcamps, and enterprises.
## Target revenue arc
The target path is not linear. The first year is proof. The middle years are offer and channel expansion. The final years require enterprise, certification, licensing, or other leverage beyond solo creator economics.
Target annual revenue milestones:
* Launch runway, May-August 2026: $0-$25K
* Age 35 year, August 2026-August 2027: $100K-$200K
* Age 36 year, August 2027-August 2028: $400K-$750K
* Age 37 year, August 2028-August 2029: $1M-$1.75M
* Age 38 year, August 2029-August 2030: $2.5M-$3.5M
* Age 39 to 40 year, August 2030-August 2031: $5M run-rate
## Strategic thesis
AI SHIFT should evolve through five business models:
1. Creator-led course and workshop business to validate demand.
2. Cohort-based training business to prove transformation and raise average order value.
3. Premium implementation and group coaching business to generate case studies and higher revenue per customer.
4. B2B training and team enablement business to unlock larger contracts.
5. Certification, licensing, and partner distribution business to scale beyond Mitchell's personal delivery capacity.
The Capstone Facilitator role is not the revenue center. It is a credibility, curriculum, and distribution lab. It gives access to real AI PM learners, project work, student friction, Marily's network, and proof that the method can help people implement.
## Year 0: Launch runway, May-August 2026
### Revenue target
$0-$25K.
### Business model
Validation wedge: free lead magnet, first workshop, first Capstone cohort, early paid test if demand appears.
### Required moves
* Send Marily the workshop artifacts and get feedback on the wedge workflow.
* Lock the workshop/lead magnet workflow.
* Ship the smallest viable lead magnet: video, one asset, landing page, delivery email.
* Configure email capture and basic tracking.
* Run a 14-day LinkedIn-to-signup test.
* Sign and deliver the first Capstone Facilitator cohort.
* Use Capstone delivery to collect real implementation problems, language, and proof.
### Revenue logic
Revenue does not matter much yet. The goal is evidence: qualified subscribers, workshop interest, Capstone delivery proof, and signs that people want a repeatable AI workflow system.
### Team
No hires. Use contractors only if they directly reduce shipping friction, such as light design, page setup, or video editing.
### Gate to proceed
Proceed only if at least one channel produces real audience signal: Marily workshop interest, subscriber conversion, Capstone learner feedback, or direct requests for more help.

## Capstone Facilitator strategy
The Capstone role should be treated as a strategic wedge, not a major revenue source.
### Year 0-Year 1
Use Capstone to learn where AI PM learners get stuck, collect language, build credibility, and strengthen the workshop offer.
### Year 2
Package a Capstone Accelerator that can be sold to bootcamps, communities, and AI education programs at $5K-$10K per cohort.
### Year 3
Turn the Capstone model into a partner offer: curriculum, facilitation, project rubrics, review templates, and AI SHIFT implementation support.
### Year 4-Year 5
Use Capstone-style delivery as one branch of certification and licensing. The value is no longer Mitchell facilitating one cohort. The value is the system others can run.
## Evidence gates
Each pivot needs evidence before expansion.
### Gate 1: Lead magnet validation
Signals: 8%-12%+ landing page conversion from relevant traffic, 20%+ asset/video engagement, 3-5 high-quality replies or DMs naming the target pain.
### Gate 2: Paid course validation
Signals: 5%-10% seed launch conversion from the list, at least 10-25 paying customers, clear testimonials, low refund rate, and evidence that customers finish enough of the material to get value.
### Gate 3: Cohort validation
Signals: 20+ students in a cohort at $1,500-$3,000, repeatable student results, and delivery that does not require Mitchell to personally rescue every participant.
### Gate 4: B2B validation
Signals: 3-5 paid team workshops, repeatable buyer language, clear ROI story, and at least one buyer asking for follow-on training.
### Gate 5: Certification/licensing validation
Signals: trained facilitators can deliver the method, outcomes remain strong, partners ask to use the system, and quality standards are enforceable.
## Team scaling principles
Do not hire to feel legitimate. Hire only when a constraint is repeated, revenue-backed, and clearly limiting growth.
### First hires or contractors
* Ops/admin support to protect Mitchell's focus.
* Editor/designer to speed content and course production.
* Contract facilitator to support cohorts.
### First full-time role
Program/operations manager, once the business crosses roughly $500K-$750K run-rate and delivery complexity is slowing growth.
### Next leadership layer
* Sales or partnerships lead once B2B is repeatable.
* Program director once curriculum and certification become major revenue lines.
* Customer success lead once student results become the brand's core asset.
### By $5M run-rate
The company likely needs 6-10 core team members plus a flexible bench of facilitators, coaches, and contractors. A solo creator model cannot reliably sustain $5M without quality or energy breaking.
## Strategic risks
### Risk 1: Overbuilding before proof
The antidote is strict evidence gates. Do not build full curriculum, certification, or licensing until the prior layer proves demand.
### Risk 2: Low-ticket trap
A $197-$497 course can validate demand, but it cannot be the long-term center of a $5M business without massive distribution. Prices must move up as outcomes become proven.
### Risk 3: Founder delivery bottleneck
If every result depends on Mitchell personally teaching, coaching, selling, and writing, the business caps early. The method must become teachable by others.
### Risk 4: Channel concentration
Marily is a strong starting channel, not a complete distribution strategy. The business needs multiple partner channels, B2B pipeline, owned email, and public content.
### Risk 5: Category dilution
AI education is crowded. The business must stay anchored to reliable AI workflows and systems over tools. Tool reviews, prompt packs, and hype content pull the brand into a weaker category.

The near-term sequence:
1. Send Marily the artifacts.

3. Ship the lead magnet.

## Strategic conclusion
The $5M goal is possible only if PLF and Capstone are treated as the first proof layer of a larger business. The path is not "sell a course to $5M." The path is: validate AI SHIFT with individuals, prove transformation in cohorts, package it for teams, certify others to deliver it, and license the method through trusted channels.
