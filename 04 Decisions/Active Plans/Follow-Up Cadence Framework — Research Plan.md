# Follow-Up Cadence Framework — Research Plan

## Why this exists
Mitchell wants a trusted follow-up framework that prevents two failure modes:
- Following up too soon because an opportunity feels big.
- Waiting too long because he does not want to seem overbearing.

## Desired output
A practical decision framework an AI agent can apply when Mitchell asks, "Should I follow up with this person yet?"

## Research scope
Use current business follow-up best practices, strategic communication norms, and relationship context. The output should be a short playbook, not a generic article.

## Inputs the framework should consider
- Relationship warmth: close collaborator, warm contact, weak tie, cold contact.
- Prior commitment: did they promise a date, ask for materials, or leave next step vague?
- Opportunity importance: high leverage does not automatically justify more pressure.
- Communication channel: email, text, Slack, LinkedIn, call.
- Time since last message.
- Whether the follow-up adds new value or just asks for attention.
- Whether silence likely means busy, unclear next step, low interest, or mismatch.

## Provisional operating rule until research is done
Do not follow up just because the opportunity feels important. Follow up when one of these is true:
1. A promised response window passed.
2. You can add new value, clarity, or a useful artifact.
3. The next step is blocked and the relationship is warm enough for a direct check-in.
4. Waiting creates real downside, not just anxiety.
