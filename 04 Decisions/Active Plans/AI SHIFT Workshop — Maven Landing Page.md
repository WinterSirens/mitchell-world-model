## WHAT YOU'LL BUILD

By the end of the session, you'll have six things:

**1. A Workflow Brief**
A precise definition of what your chosen workflow should produce — who it's for, what format it takes, what "done" looks like, and what "not done" looks like. The brief most people skip is the reason most AI outputs fall apart.

**2. A Context Pack**
The reusable material that stops AI from producing generic output — your best and worst examples, style rules, source material, and constraints. This is what makes AI sound like you instead of everyone else.

**3. A Failure Map**
A structured map of where your workflow is likely to go wrong — hallucination risks, formatting failures, voice drift, and edge cases. Most AI workflows fail at the same five points. You'll know all of them before you ship.

**4. A Test Set**
3–5 real inputs — easy, messy, and edge-case — to validate the workflow before you trust it with real work.

**5. A Reusable AI Handoff v1**
A prompt, Claude Project, Custom GPT, or structured instruction set built around your actual task. Not a template someone else wrote. Yours — configured, tested, and ready to use.

**6. A 7-Day SHIFT Plan**
A simple measurement and improvement roadmap for the week after the workshop, so your handoff keeps improving instead of sitting in a folder.

## HOW THE WORKSHOP RUNS

The session is a live build alongside a working demo.

Mitchell builds a complete AI workflow live — taking one messy recurring task from raw input to reliable output — while you build a workflow for your own task in parallel. Every section produces a real artifact. There are no slides-only sections. You are building for the full 3 hours.

The demo task will be chosen based on what resonates most with this audience. If you can't think of your own task, you can build alongside the demo.

**Session arc:**

- **Frame (15 min):** Why AI output is inconsistent — and what's actually causing it
- **Pick (20 min):** Select and score one recurring task using the Wedge Scorecard
- **S — Specify (30 min):** Build a Workflow Brief — define the output before you involve AI
- **H — Harvest (30 min):** Build a Context Pack — the reusable material that stops generic output
- *Break (10 min)*
- **I — Inspect (25 min):** Map failure modes and write 3–5 test inputs
- **F — Feed (30 min):** Run your workflow on real inputs, see what breaks, make your first refinement
- **T — Telemetry (15 min):** Set up a 7-Day SHIFT Plan; preview the 6-Gear roadmap
- **Close (5 min):** CTAs — newsletter, course waitlist, workflow teardown submissions

All templates are provided as fillable Google Docs at the start of the session.

## FAQ

**What AI tool do I need to bring?**
Any tool you already use — ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Gemini, or anything else. The SHIFT Method is tool-agnostic. You'll build your workflow in the tool you're already comfortable with.

**Do I need any technical or coding skills?**
None. If you can use Google Docs and you've opened an AI chatbot before, you have everything you need. This is not an engineering course.

**What task should I bring?**
A real recurring task from your actual work — something you do at least weekly, that currently takes 30+ minutes, and that produces a document, piece of content, or analysis as its output. The more specific and recurring it is, the better. If you can't think of one, you can build alongside the demo task Mitchell runs live.

**How is this different from learning to write better prompts?**
A better prompt only solves one instance. This workshop builds the system behind the prompt — defining the output, gathering the context AI actually needs, mapping where it's likely to fail, and testing it systematically. The handoff system you build today will keep working when the tool you're using updates or gets replaced by a new one.
