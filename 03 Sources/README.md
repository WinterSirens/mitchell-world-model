## What this folder is
Preserved upstream material: articles, meetings, podcasts, chats, and research notes whose original form is likely to be useful again.

This is not a second archive. The default workflow is **distill, then archive**. Preserve a source here only when you can name why a future agent or Mitchell would re-read the original.

## When to preserve a source
- The methodology, transcript, or evidence may need to be re-examined.
- The source supports an active decision or research thread.
- The original contains details that would be too lossy to compress into a 30–50 line Wiki note.
- The source is likely to be cited, quoted, or used as training material again.

## When not to preserve a source
- A short Wiki or World Model distillation fully captures the useful delta.
- The source was merely inspirational or contextual.
- The source is generic advice with no durable action change.
- Keeping it would make retrieval noisier.

## Subfolders
- `Articles/` — preserved articles and clippings
- `Podcasts/` — podcast notes or transcripts worth reusing
- `Meetings/` — meeting notes worth preserving as source material
- `Chats/` — notable AI conversation outputs worth revisiting
- `Research Notes/` — deeper research artifacts backing decisions
