# Scripture Study — Fuel, Not Preparation

## Core principle

Studying the scriptures is not about gathering material to sound convincing. It's about letting Christ become convincing in me. Influence comes after personal conversion, not before.

- Old mindset: "I read so I have something to say."
- New mindset: "I read so Christ can change me, and then my words carry His power."

## Supporting references

- **Alma 32:28** — The word swells and enlarges the soul over time. Growth is like tending a plant: consistent, quiet care. Consistency over novelty.
- **D&C 11:21 / 112:21** — Obtain the word first, then your tongue is loosed. Study powers and fuels influence rather than just being a preparation step.

## When study feels flat

- Bring one specific real-life need to the Lord each day.
- Ask: "How does this passage help me come closer to Christ in that area?"
- Let the word swell slowly — less about novelty, more about faithful small steps.
