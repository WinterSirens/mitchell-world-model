# Leadership — Process Over Winning

## Core principle

The most successful leaders don't fixate on winning. They focus on the process — daily habits, core values, incremental improvements — and winning follows. Define success by the standard of work being done today, not the outcome expected tomorrow.

## Proactive appreciation

"What you look for, you find." Catch people doing things right. Go beyond generic "good job" — offer substantive feedback that shows you're paying attention to the small things. Gratitude builds resilience. Toughness without appreciation breeds fear, not performance.

## Standards for mentorship

Mentorship is two-way. Before taking on a mentee, student, or client, define the price of admission: work ethic, receptiveness to feedback, accountability. These are prerequisites, not things you hope develop along the way.

## Develop the whole person

Don't just build technical skill — build character. Integrity, teamwork, and support for peers are mandatory prerequisites for participation and promotion, not optional extras.
