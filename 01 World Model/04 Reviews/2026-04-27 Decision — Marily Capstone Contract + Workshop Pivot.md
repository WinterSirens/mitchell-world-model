# Decision — Marily Capstone Contract + Workshop Pivot

## Situation
The 2026-04-28 meeting with Marily happened a day early on 2026-04-27. Two things came out of it:

1. **Capstone Facilitator contract.** Marily offered a $3,000/cohort stipend to stay on as a Capstone Facilitator for the AI PM Bootcamp, helping participants with their Capstone projects. Contract to be sent later this week.
2. **Workshop opening.** Marily is open to running a workshop for the AI SHIFT program in her network. She asked Mitchell to put together a Google Doc or NotebookLM presentation explaining exactly what the program is.

Both are upstream of any lead magnet conversion. The workshop in particular is a real distribution test through a warm, qualified audience.

## Decision
1. **Accept the Capstone Facilitator contract** when it arrives. Sign and return promptly.
2. **Pivot the active bottleneck** from "ship the smallest viable lead magnet" to "design and pitch a workshop to Marily for her network." Lead magnet remains a close follow — both still depend on the wedge-workflow decision.

## Reasoning
- The contract is a defined, bounded commitment with clear scope and direct compensation. It does not threaten focus and reinforces the Marily relationship.
- The workshop is a higher-leverage way to test the AI SHIFT method than LinkedIn organic + lead magnet alone. It puts the program in front of a warm, AI-curious audience with Marily's implicit endorsement.
- Both paths require the same upstream decision — choosing the wedge workflow — so the wedge decision is still the true unblocker.
- This is exactly the override case the Opportunity filter contemplates: rare, meaningfully aligned, replaces a lower-value activity (slow LinkedIn list-building) rather than stacking on top.

## Constraints applied
- Opportunity filter: passes — it directly improves the bottleneck (audience access) and unlocks distribution that is hard to get otherwise.
- Lead magnet sequencing rule: not invalidated. The smallest viable lead magnet is still the right shape; it just isn't the immediate leading edge anymore.
- Capacity: the Capstone Facilitator role adds a recurring commitment. Need to check this against Capacity Management context before signing.

## Counter-arguments to hold honestly
- **Validation seeking.** Therapy session this week named the pattern of wanting validation. The Marily relationship feels rewarding partly because she gives back. Worth asking: am I pivoting because the workshop is genuinely the better path, or because it feels good to be wanted? Test: would I still pivot if Marily were neutral and the workshop was offered by a stranger with the same network? Probably yes — distribution leverage is real — but flag it.
- **Founder-led detour.** Building a workshop deck and a relationship-driven pitch is not the same as shipping a public lead magnet. If the workshop doesn't materialize quickly, the lead magnet path will have lost weeks.
- **SF trip idea.** The note mentions potentially flying to SF to "build something incredible together" with Marily. This is a separate decision and should not be folded into the workshop pitch. Park it in `New Opportunity` framing if it comes up again.

## Strategic reframe (added after synthesis)
Mitchell reframed the workshop from "pitch to get permission" into **a paid mini seed launch.** The workshop generates real revenue, surfaces real pain points from a paying audience, and lets Mitchell SHIFT the system toward what actually causes pain in AI implementations. That audience signal is more valuable than any amount of pre-launch LinkedIn posting.

The wedge workflow can be passed into the high-level pitch as a candidate — not decided in a vacuum first. Send Marily a v1 that includes a candidate workflow, get her feedback on what resonates, and then refine. The pitch artifact is a feedback loop, not a dead-end deliverable.
