## Overcommitment signals

- More than 6 active commitments
- The primary bottleneck hasn't moved in over a week
- Saying "I'll get to it" about the same thing multiple sessions in a row
- New commitments are being added without anything being completed or dropped
- Energy is consistently low at the start of work sessions
- Context-switching between projects within a single work block

## Triage protocol

When overcommitment signals appear:

1. **List everything.** Write out every active commitment (update the list above).
2. **Identify the bottleneck.** Which one commitment, if completed, unblocks the most value? That's the priority.
3. **Classify the rest:**
   - **Keep** — directly supports the bottleneck or has a hard external deadline
   - **Pause** — valuable but not urgent. Set a specific revisit date.
   - **Drop** — was a good idea when you said yes, but the cost of carrying it is now higher than the benefit
4. **Apply the opportunity filter rule:** "If commitments are already high, require an explicit tradeoff before adding something new."
5. **Name what you're not doing.** Unspoken commitments create guilt drag. If something is paused or dropped, say so explicitly.
