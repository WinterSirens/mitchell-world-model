## Project Overview
Building an AI education business for "Stuck Implementers" — tech-comfortable professionals who've tried AI but can't get consistent, meaningful results.

**Core product:** The SHIFT Gear System course, teaching people to take any task from fully manual to fully autonomous using a repeatable framework.

**Entry point:** The AI SHIFT Method lead magnet — a free kit that teaches someone to build their first AI tool (a Claude Project for a professional workflow) using the SHIFT Method.

**Distribution:** LinkedIn content (3-4x/week) driving email newsletter signups. Seed launch happens when list hits 300-400 subscribers.

## The AI SHIFT Method (Lead Magnet)

### The Framework
- **S** — Specify the problem
- **H** — Harvest the context
- **I** — Inspect for gaps
- **F** — Feed and test
- **T** — Telemetry (metrics, monitoring, feedback)

### Lead Magnet Kit Components
| Component | Status |
|---|---|
| Guided video (8-10 min) | Not yet recorded |
| Pre-built system prompt (annotated) | Drafted in Notion |
| Quick-Start Reference Card (1 page) | Not yet designed |
| Email 1-5 nurture sequence | Outlined, not fully drafted |
| Landing page HTML | Draft exists (Claude Code ready) |

### Nurture Email Sequence (Post-Signup)
- **Email 1 (Immediate):** Deliver the kit. Short, warm, zero selling.
- **Email 2 (Day 2):** Google Calendar connector upgrade — "Supercharge Your SHIFT"
- **Email 3 (Day 4):** Second SHIFT — new pre-built system prompt for a different workflow
- **Email 4 (Day 6):** Why AI gives bad answers (H and F steps deep dive)
- **Email 5 (Day 8-9):** Menu of 2-3 additional pre-built system prompts

**Note:** This sequence is value-delivery only. No sales offer until 300-400 subscriber threshold is reached.

## The SHIFT Gear System (Course)

### Six Gears
| Gear | Name | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Manual | You do the work. AI assists at the margins. |
| 2 | Collaborator | AI works alongside you in a Claude Project. |
| 3 | Delegator | AI drafts, you review and approve. |
| 4 | Automated | Process runs on a schedule without you starting it. |
| 5 | Autonomous (Human in the loop) | AI executes, you approve before it ships. |
| 6 | Autonomous (Human on the loop) | AI ships, you monitor metrics and intervene on exceptions. |

### Key principle
SHIFT is applied at every gear. You cannot skip gears. Each gear requires the output of the previous gear as input.

### Lead magnet to course relationship
Lead magnet teaches SHIFT at Gear 1 on one task. Course reveals the full Gear System and shows how to take any task through all 6 gears.

### Course promise
"Stop being someone who tries individual AI tools and start being someone who builds an AI-augmented workflow for your role."

### Open course questions
- Use all 6 gears or simplify to 5 for v1 (Gears 5/6 distinction may be advanced content)
- Number of worked examples per gear (recommendation: 2-3 across different professional contexts)
- Pricing tier (audience data supports $197-$3,000 depending on format)

## Launch Threshold Rule
**The seed launch cannot happen until the email list reaches 300-400 subscribers.**

Until that threshold:
- Focus is entirely on list growth and lead magnet delivery
- Do not build course content ahead of audience
- Do not pitch the course or tease a launch date
