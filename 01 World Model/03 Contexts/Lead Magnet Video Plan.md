## Decision: locked

**Wedge:** LinkedIn content creation using the SHIFT method
**Input:** Unstructured word vomit (voice dumps on a walk)
**Output:** Polished LinkedIn posts in Notion, ready to schedule
**Method shown:** SHIFT — taught as the strategy used to build the system, not as a lecture

## One-line promise

"I'll show you the system I built to go from word vomit on a walk to a full month of LinkedIn content in 15 minutes — and give you the strategy to build your own."

## Transformation statement

Most people: sit at a desk, stare at a blank post, wrestle with AI for 20 minutes, get something generic, give up or post it anyway.

You: go for a walk, think out loud, come home to 3 polished posts waiting in Notion. Once a week, 15 minutes to review and schedule. Done.

## Video structure (8-10 min)

### Cold open (75 sec)
Start with the walk. Not the AI. Not the framework.

> "I create all my LinkedIn content on my walks. No desk. No blank page. No 45-minute battle with a draft that still doesn't sound right. I walk, I think out loud, and by the time I get home, three posts are ready in my content database. Once a week, 15 minutes to review and schedule everything. That's my content operation."

Pause. *"Here's how I built it."*

### The SHIFT strategy — fast (90 sec)
Frame it as: "Before I built this, I had to answer five questions."

- **S** — *What am I actually trying to produce?*
- **H** — *What does the system need to know about me to do this right?*
- **I** — *What's missing? What would make the output worse?*
- **F** — *Test it small. Does it sound like me?*
- **T** — *When it's running — what do I watch? When do I iterate?*

### Transfer of learning (45 sec)
> "I built the email version first. Refined it. Then I applied the same five questions to LinkedIn. Didn't start from scratch. The learning transferred. That's the point of having a method."

### Live demo (3-4 min)
Show the actual pipeline:
1. A raw thought dump — something genuinely messy, like you'd say mid-walk
2. The output that appears in Notion
3. The one or two edits you make
4. Run it again. One more thought → another post. Show the speed.

### The full picture (45 sec)
> "Once a week. 15 minutes. I load everything up, review, and schedule. My whole content calendar is done before most people have written their first draft."

### What's next — the gear hook (30 sec)
> "The next version I'm building will review and publish automatically. I won't even need the 15 minutes. That's SHIFT continuing to work — you don't just get faster, you eventually get out of the loop entirely."

*This seeds the course without pitching it. Don't explain further. Let them want to know how.*

### Close + asset (45 sec)
> "I'm not giving you a prompt to copy. I'm giving you the strategy I used to build this — because a prompt only works for me. The strategy lets you build something that works for you. Link below."

## Asset spec

**Name:** The SHIFT Content System — Build Your Own
**Format:** One page, five sections + one diagram

| Section | Content |
|---|---|
| S | What does your best content actually do? Define the outcome before you build. |
| H | What does the system need to know? Voice, tone, goals, examples, no-go rules. |
| I | What would make it wrong? What's missing from what you've gathered? |
| F | Test with one real thought. Would you post this? What needs to change? |
| T | What are you watching? When will you refine it? |

**Diagram:** Walk → word vomit → AI → Notion database → weekly review → scheduled posts

**What it is NOT:** A prompt to copy. The strategy to build their own.

## Recording prep checklist

- [ ] Pick a real word vomit example — something genuinely messy, like you'd say on a walk. The messier the better; it makes the transformation more dramatic.
- [ ] Have Notion open and ready with the content database visible
- [ ] Run the demo live at least once before recording so the flow is smooth
- [ ] Have 2-3 additional thought dumps ready to run quickly during the "speed" section
- [ ] Do not script the cold open — just say the walk story naturally
